Main concept:
This is not a traditional LMS. It is an Agentic AI platform where AI is the operating system of the learning journey. The learner does not start with a course catalogue. The learner is greeted by a personal AI Capability Agent that understands their role, builds a dynamic capability profile, creates a personalised development mission, adapts the journey, guides learning, supports AI experimentation, helps create an AI Digital Twin, and connects learning to Outcome Projects.

The platform should demonstrate outcome-based learning, not progress-based learning. Success is measured by capability demonstrated, projects delivered, value created, hours saved, productivity improved, and service impact — not only course completions.
