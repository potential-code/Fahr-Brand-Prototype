Screen by screen, with what goes on each. This is the spine of the mockup.

1. Login
SSO button + UAE PASS button, Arabic/English toggle, FAHR branding.

2. Profile setup
Name, entity, department, job role, seniority. Learning objectives. Consent/policy acknowledgement checkbox. Progress stepper.

3. Baseline assessment
Diagnostic quiz — scenario-based questions, progress bar, question counter. Ends with a results screen: competency scores (radar or bars), strengths vs gaps.

4. AI Skills Advisor result
"Based on your assessment…" — named gaps, recommended development priorities, mapped to FAHR competencies. This is the handoff into the pathway.

5. Learner dashboard ← the hero screen

Assigned pathway with % progress
Recommended next activity ("AI suggests…")
Upcoming events/webinars
Announcements
Certificates and badges earned
Points / leaderboard rank
Persistent AI Concierge launcher (corner bubble)

6. Pathway view
Ordered modules with lock/unlock states, mixed formats visible: self-paced course, webinar, microlearning video, SCORM, practical assignment. Competency tag on each.

7. Course player
Video or content pane, progress, notes — plus AI Learning Coach side panel: ask questions, get explanations, resource suggestions.

8. AI Practice Partner
Role-play / workplace scenario. Chat interface, learner responds to a simulated situation, gets feedback on their answer.

9. Assessment + adaptive moment
Knowledge check → score → pathway visibly updates. Show a "your pathway has been adjusted" state. This is the single most important screen — it proves the adaptive claim in §5.2.

10. Certificate
Digital certificate, FAHR-branded, competencies achieved, shareable/downloadable.

11. AI Digital Twin (§6.2)
Setup wizard: role, org context, frequent tasks, working style, goals, knowledge sources. Then a chat screen where the trained twin assists with a work task.

12. Community / engagement
Discussion forum, leaderboard, polls, event registration.

13. My progress
Competency growth over time, completions, assessment history.

The loop to make visible: assessment → AI reads it → pathway personalises → learner progresses → new assessment → pathway adjusts.
