Potential FZ-LLC, trading as Potential.com, proposes a single, secure, scalable, bilingual, and Agentic AI-enabled platform that brings the complete journey together within one FAHR-branded experience:
onboarding and learner profiling;

2. Understanding of the UAE Federal Agentic AI Skilling Context
2.1 The Next Phase of Government Workforce Transformation
Artificial Intelligence is reshaping how governments operate, influencing policy design, service delivery, decision-making, and citizen engagement. Globally, public sector organisations are moving beyond traditional digital transformation towards AI-enabled operating models that prioritise agility, intelligence, and responsiveness. Within this evolution, Agentic AI—systems capable of autonomous reasoning, decision support, and collaborative interaction—represents a significant advancement beyond conventional automation.
For the UAE Federal Government, this transition aligns with national ambitions to lead in innovation, digital governance, and AI adoption. As AI becomes embedded in everyday workflows, the role of public sector employees is evolving from system users to active collaborators with intelligent technologies. This shift requires new competencies in critical thinking, AI interaction, ethical judgement, and continuous learning.
FAHR’s initiative therefore represents more than the introduction of AI tools or awareness programmes. It is a strategic effort to build workforce capability at scale—enabling employees to confidently and responsibly apply AI to enhance productivity, improve service delivery, and support national priorities. Achieving this requires a fundamental shift in how learning is delivered, how skills are developed, and how performance is measured.
Through this initiative, FAHR is positioning the UAE Federal Government at the forefront of workforce transformation, ensuring readiness for both current and future technological advancements.
2.2 From AI Awareness to Workforce Capability
Traditional workforce development has largely focused on structured learning through courses, certifications, and assessments. While these approaches remain important for foundational knowledge, they are insufficient to develop practical AI capability in a rapidly evolving environment.
Agentic AI adoption requires a transition from knowledge acquisition to applied capability. Employees must be able to use AI in real-world contexts, solve practical challenges, and continuously refine their skills. This progression—from awareness to application and mastery—cannot be achieved through static learning models alone.
Effective AI skilling programmes must therefore deliver continuous, adaptive learning embedded within the flow of work. This includes personalised learning journeys aligned to roles and skill levels, real-time guidance, hands-on simulations, and opportunities for safe experimentation. Equally important are mechanisms to track progress, measure outcomes, and provide actionable feedback.
Given the pace of technological change, workforce capability must also be continuously updated. This requires a dynamic learning ecosystem capable of evolving content, tools, and guidance in line with emerging technologies and organisational priorities.
FAHR’s initiative reflects this shift, recognising that AI capability development is an ongoing process requiring sustained engagement, intelligent support, and measurable outcomes.
2.3 Key Success Factors for a Federal Agentic AI Programme
Based on our experience delivering large-scale workforce transformation programmes, we have identified the following critical success factors:
Personalised and Role-Based Learning Journeys: Tailored pathways aligned to job functions, skill levels, and organisational priorities to ensure relevance and engagement.
AI-Powered Guidance and Continuous Coaching: Intelligent support providing real-time recommendations and feedback to enable effective learning and application.
Practical Application and Experiential Learning: Hands-on simulations and real-world scenarios that enable employees to apply AI in meaningful contexts.
Integrated Analytics and Workforce Insights: Comprehensive visibility into skill development, adoption, and organisational readiness to support informed decision-making.
Embedded Responsible AI Governance: Built-in frameworks ensuring ethical, secure, and compliant AI usage, with transparency and accountability.
Scalability and Multi-Entity Support: A flexible and scalable solution capable of supporting up to 80,000 employees across diverse federal entities.
Change Management and Adoption Enablement: Structured engagement and communication strategies to drive adoption and sustain participation.
Continuous Evolution: The ability to update capabilities, integrate new technologies, and incorporate emerging best practices over time.
2.4 Potential.com's Understanding
Potential.com recognises that FAHR is establishing a national capability platform rather than procuring a conventional Learning Management System. The objective is to enable up to 80,000 federal employees to develop practical, applied, and continuously evolving Agentic AI capabilities.
This requires an integrated solution that combines learning, application, analytics, and governance within a unified ecosystem. It must support both individual capability development and organisational oversight, ensuring alignment with federal priorities.
Our approach is based on embedding AI capability development within the flow of work, supported by intelligent technologies and aligned with operational objectives. We also recognise the importance of providing FAHR with the tools and insights required to manage workforce transformation at scale.
Accordingly, our solution is designed to empower federal employees to confidently and responsibly adopt AI, while equipping FAHR and participating entities with the governance, intelligence, and operational capabilities needed to deliver measurable and sustainable impact.
This understanding forms the foundation of the integrated solution presented in the following sections.
2.5 Potential.com’s Interpretation of Success
Potential.com defines success as the measurable transformation of workforce capability and organisational performance across the UAE Federal Government.
At the individual level, success is demonstrated when employees confidently integrate Agentic AI into their daily work—enhancing productivity, improving decision-making, and delivering higher-quality services. This includes the ability to identify opportunities for AI application, collaborate effectively with intelligent systems, and adapt to evolving technologies.
At the organisational level, success is reflected in improved efficiency, faster service delivery, and stronger policy outcomes supported by data-driven insights. FAHR and participating entities will have clear visibility into workforce capability through real-time analytics, enabling proactive management and alignment with national priorities.
Sustainability is equally critical. The platform must become an integral part of the federal workforce operating model—widely adopted, continuously evolving, and embedded within a culture of learning and innovation. Responsible AI practices must also be consistently upheld to ensure trust, compliance, and ethical use.
Ultimately, success is the establishment of a future-ready federal workforce that is not only capable of using AI, but is empowered to lead with it—positioning the UAE as a global benchmark in government-led AI capability development.

6. Optional AI Sandbox Response – The Agentic AI Lab
6.1 Overview
As a value-added capability, Potential.com proposes the Agentic AI Lab—a secure, governed experimentation environment that enables federal employees to safely experience, explore, and apply Agentic AI within a controlled learning environment.
The Agentic AI Lab is designed to support FAHR's learning objectives by allowing employees to experiment with AI in practical, real-world scenarios without exposing production systems or sensitive government data. It is not intended to function as an agent-development platform; rather, it provides a structured environment where learners progressively build confidence, apply newly acquired skills, and explore Agentic AI concepts through guided activities and simulations, fully aligned with the RFP's intent.
The Agentic AI Lab follows a phased maturity model, allowing FAHR to introduce increasingly advanced capabilities over time while maintaining governance, security, and operational simplicity.
6.2 Phase 1 – Build Your AI Digital Twin (Available Today)
The first stage of the Agentic AI Lab is available today within Potential.com's production platform and will be configured for FAHR as part of the proposed solution.
Rather than asking employees to build AI agents from scratch, learners are guided through the creation of their own AI Digital Twin—a trusted AI companion that understands their role, responsibilities, working context, preferred communication style, and day-to-day activities.
Throughout the learning programme, employees progressively train their Digital Twin by defining:
Their job role and responsibilities.
Organisational context and business domain.
Frequently performed tasks and workflows.
Preferred working style and communication preferences.
Personal objectives and development goals.
Knowledge sources and approved reference materials.
As the learner progresses through the programme, the AI Digital Twin becomes increasingly capable of supporting everyday work by providing contextual guidance, assisting with routine activities, answering questions, generating content, and acting as a personalised productivity assistant aligned with the employee's role.
This creates a highly engaging learning experience where employees immediately apply newly acquired AI skills while developing an AI companion that continues to deliver value beyond the training programme.
6.3 Future Phase – Agentic Workflow Studio (Roadmap)
As FAHR's AI maturity evolves, the Agentic AI Lab can be extended through an optional second phase introducing an Agentic Workflow Studio. This capability is part of Potential.com's product roadmap and can be configured as a future enhancement if required.
Within this environment, employees move beyond individual AI assistants to designing complete Agentic AI workflows capable of supporting end-to-end business processes.
Using a guided visual interface, authorised learners can safely experiment with workflows that combine multiple AI capabilities to address practical workplace challenges such as:
HR and employee services.
Internal knowledge assistance.
Policy interpretation.
Customer and stakeholder support.
Productivity automation.
Document processing.
Administrative workflows.
Government service scenarios.
These workflows are created exclusively within the governed sandbox environment and are intended for learning, experimentation, and proof-of-concept activities rather than direct production deployment.
6.4 Safe, Secure and Governed Experimentation
The Agentic AI Lab has been specifically designed as an isolated experimentation environment that supports learning while maintaining enterprise-grade governance and security.
Key capabilities include:
Complete isolation from production systems and operational government data.
Configurable simulated government scenarios.
Role-based permissions and access controls.
Prompt and response logging.
AI guardrails and safety controls.
Content moderation and abuse monitoring.
Reset and replay functionality for repeated learning exercises.

These controls allow employees to experiment confidently while enabling FAHR to maintain visibility, compliance, and responsible AI governance throughout the learning experience.
